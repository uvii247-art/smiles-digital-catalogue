## Finding the first 20 prospects

1. Make a list of 20 Nigerian businesses on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status and Google Maps: fashion vendors, skincare sellers, gift shops, food vendors, hair vendors and homeware sellers.
2. Prioritise businesses with good products but no website, an outdated bio link, or a difficult-to-browse WhatsApp catalogue.
3. Personalise the opening line with one specific product or positive observation.
4. Send five thoughtful messages per day for four days; do not spam or bulk-message strangers.
5. Offer a free 3-product preview, not a full free website.
6. Follow up once after 2–3 days with a screenshot or a short product suggestion.
7. Track name, niche, contact, date contacted, reply, next step and outcome in a simple spreadsheet.
8. Ask every interested business for its top three products, brand colours and WhatsApp number.
9. Close with a clear scope, delivery date and 50% deposit before custom work.
10. After delivery, ask for a testimonial and one referral.

## Simple delivery process

1. **Discovery:** collect logo, brand colours, product photos, names, descriptions, prices, sizes/colours, delivery areas and WhatsApp number.
2. **Quote:** confirm package, number of products, revision count and delivery date in writing.
3. **Deposit:** request a 50% deposit before starting custom work; do not purchase tools or subscriptions for the customer.
4. **Build:** replace sample products, update contact details and test every product link.
5. **Preview:** send a private preview and allow the agreed revisions.
6. **Final approval:** confirm that the customer has checked prices, product availability, phone number and delivery information.
7. **Handover:** send the live link, the repository link if included, and short instructions for requesting updates.
8. **Balance and support:** collect the balance, then provide the agreed support period.

## Customer content checklist

- Business name and logo
- Short About Us paragraph
- WhatsApp number with country code
- Email and social links
- 12–60 product photos
- Product names, descriptions and prices
- Size/colour options
- Delivery locations and fees
- Accepted payment methods
- Return/exchange policy
- Preferred domain or free hosting choice
